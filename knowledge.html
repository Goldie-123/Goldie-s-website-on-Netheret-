Helpful IT Guides

How do I restart my Wi-Fi router?

Switch off your router using its power button. Wait approximately 30 seconds, then switch it back on. Allow a few minutes for the connection to return.

What should I do if my printer is not printing?

Check that the printer is switched on, has paper and ink, and is connected to your computer or network. Check the print queue for any stuck jobs.

What should I do if an application freezes?

Wait briefly to see if it responds. If it remains frozen, save any available work and restart the application. Check for software updates if the problem continues.

How can I create a strong and stable password?

Use a long, unique password containing a mixture of words, numbers and symbols. Do not share your password with anyone, keep it to yourself and write it in a book or autosave the password in Google or edge.
